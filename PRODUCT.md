# AgencyOS Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Agency founders and authorized operations, finance, legal, HR, delivery, and client-service staff use AgencyOS during daily administrative and decision-making work. They need to find reliable records quickly, understand who can access them, and complete operational workflows without switching between disconnected tools.

## Product Purpose

AgencyOS is a private operating system for an agency. It keeps commercial, delivery, finance, people, legal, vendor, asset, support, reporting, automation, and AI workflows connected to the same organization and permission model. Success means that a permitted user can locate the current source of truth, understand its history and relationships, and act on it with an auditable trail.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and efficient. The product should feel precise and dependable during sensitive operational work, with clear language and familiar controls that stay out of the user's way.

## Anti-references

- A decorative consumer file manager that hides record metadata or business relationships.
- A generic dashboard made from interchangeable cards with weak hierarchy.
- Dense enterprise software that requires users to memorize codes or navigate unexplained menus.
- AI interfaces that expose every tool at once, obscure authorization, or make unsupported claims about private records.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the source of truth: link documents and evidence to their business records instead of creating unexplained copies.
2. Make organization visible: paths, breadcrumbs, metadata, relationships, ownership, and history should answer where a record belongs.
3. Reveal complexity progressively: start with the task and load advanced controls or AI capabilities only when they are relevant.
4. Keep sensitive work legible: permissions, classifications, retention, scanning, and approval states must be understandable at a glance.
5. Favor traceable answers: AI results should identify the records, paths, versions, and metadata used without exposing unauthorized file contents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen-reader labels, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
